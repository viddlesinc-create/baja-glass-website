Roughly **15,000 of those impressions are "near me" searches** ("shower door installation
near me" 5,515, "shower door replacement near me" 3,945, plus ~30 variants) sitting at
organic positions 24–72. "Near me" clicks go almost entirely to the **Map Pack**, not to
web pages — so this traffic can only be won through the Google Business Profile.

- Rotate the high-demand phrases as post topics: shower door installation, shower door
  replacement, custom shower enclosures, walk-in showers. Always end with
  "Free in-home estimate — (702) 383-0779."

  - "Do you replace old shower doors?" → Yes — full replacement with new custom glass

  - "Do you build custom walk-in enclosures?" → Yes.
  - "Do you serve Henderson / Green Valley / Anthem?" → Yes.

  https://bajaglass.com/contact
